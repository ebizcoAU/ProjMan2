ProjMan - SaaS Website Development Prompt
High-Level Design Brief
Project Overview
Create a modern, professional SaaS website for ProjMan - an 18-Stage Construction Lifecycle platform that transforms project management and workforce credentialing in the Australian construction industry. The website should communicate trust, innovation, and industry authority while converting visitors into leads and customers.

Core Value Proposition
"ProjMan transforms the construction lifecycle into an immutable instrument of workforce integrity, replacing subjective employer testimony with multi-party verified on-site evidence."

Key Differentiators
Verifiable Career Pathways - Automated, geolocated evidence collection

RPL Transformation - Slashes credentialing time from 18 months to weeks

Multi-Party Verification - "Tick-Verify" chain with zero-trust hierarchy

VeriTrade Integration - Australia's first B2B professional network for trades

Digital Inclusion - Accessible for workers with varying digital literacy

Target Audience
Primary
Investors - Looking for scalable B2B SaaS opportunities

State Government Policy-Makers - Seeking workforce integrity solutions

Construction Companies - Tier-1 contractors, commercial builders

Secondary
Tradespeople - Seeking portable, verifiable credentials

RTOs - Looking for verifiable evidence for assessments

Project Managers - Seeking operational efficiency
